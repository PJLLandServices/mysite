Two Forest Hills, one approach.

Forest Hill isn't one lot type. The estates along Russell Hill Road, Old Forest Hill Road and Dunvegan are some of the largest residential properties in central Toronto. The lots through Forest Hill Village, around Eglinton and Spadina, are tighter — still premium, but the irrigation plan looks very different. Our job on either is the same: design something that fits the property, runs efficiently, and disappears into the landscape.

Estate

Russell Hill / Old Forest Hill

0.4 to 1 acre+ lots backing onto the Cedarvale Ravine or the Beltline. Full multi-zone designs (8-14 zones typical), pressure-regulated heads on slope, dedicated drip overlay zones for long hedge runs and beds, often paired with a landscape lighting install through the same trench.

Village core

Forest Hill Village

Tighter premium lots through the streets around Spadina and Eglinton. 4-7 zone systems, smaller designed footprints, often the priority is replacing an aging 1990s install with something that runs quietly and connects to a phone. Discreet head placement is the recurring brief.

Hedge lines

Drip overlay retrofits

Most existing Forest Hill systems were installed lawn-only; the cedar and boxwood hedges were watered by hand or not at all. A drip overlay zone tied into the existing controller is a one-day retrofit on most lots — and saves both the hedge and the water that used to overspray onto the sidewalk.

Canopy work

Heritage tree routing

Forest Hill canopy is significant. Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 658 protects any private tree with a trunk diameter of 30 cm or more — trenching through the protected root zone can require a permit. We walk the property before quoting and route around protected trees. More pipe, intact canopy.

What we check on a Forest Hill spring opening

  • ✓ Slow re-pressurize the mainline to protect older fittings on systems 20-plus years in the ground
  • ✓ Test each zone individually for pressure, coverage and rotation — including drip overlay zones, which need a separate pressure check
  • ✓ Walk hedge drip lines to confirm emitters are still seated under mulch

Forest Hill sprinkler FAQs

What kind of properties does PJL work in Forest Hill?

Forest Hill lots range from very large estates along Russell Hill Road and Old Forest Hill Road to tighter premium lots in and around Forest Hill Village. The common factors across both ends of that spectrum are mature trees, hedge-lined property edges (cedar, boxwood, hornbeam), and homeowners who want infrastructure to disappear into the landscape. Our work here is a mix of full designed systems on the larger lots, hedge-line drip retrofits, repairs to existing 1990s-2000s systems, and integrated landscape lighting installs.

Do you do drip irrigation along Forest Hill hedge lines?

Yes, this is one of our most-requested services in Forest Hill. Long cedar and boxwood hedges that define the property edge benefit from a dedicated drip overlay zone — low pressure, low flow, hidden under mulch, delivered to the root zone where the hedge actually needs it. No spray on the sidewalk or the next-door property's hardscape. Independent program from the lawn zones. See our drip irrigation page for more.

Can you integrate sprinkler installation with landscape lighting in Forest Hill?

Yes — this is one of our two service lines, and Forest Hill is where the integration pays off most. When we are installing or replacing a sprinkler mainline, we can route landscape lighting cable in the same trench. LED, marine-grade fixtures, app-controlled. The single-trench install saves the customer the cost of opening the lawn twice. See our landscape lighting page for details.

How does Toronto's tree by-law affect Forest Hill irrigation work?

Forest Hill's mature canopy means many properties have multiple trees protected under Toronto's Private Tree By-law (Municipal Code Chapter 658) — which applies to any private tree with a trunk diameter of 30 cm or more. Trenching inside the protected root zone can require a permit. We walk the property before quoting, identify the protected trees, and route the mainline and laterals to stay clear of the root zones. More pipe, intact canopy, no surprise visit from the tree-protection officer.

How quickly can you respond to a Forest Hill repair call?

Forest Hill is roughly 55-65 minutes from our Newmarket base depending on traffic. We schedule Toronto repair calls on a priority basis rather than a same-day guarantee — most jobs land within 1-3 business days during peak season (April through October). Same-day routing is a York Region commitment (Newmarket, Aurora, King City, Richmond Hill); Toronto is next-available with priority slots so you get a real arrival window, not an optimistic guess.

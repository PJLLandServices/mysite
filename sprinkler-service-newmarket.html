What's Included in a PJL Spring Opening — Newmarket

Every spring opening in Newmarket includes a full 10-point system check before we leave your property:

  • ✓ Slow-pressurize the main supply line to prevent water-hammer damage to PVC fittings
  • ✓ Test each zone individually for correct pressure, coverage, and rotation
  • ✓ Check every sprinkler head for winter damage, misalignment, or clogged nozzles
  • ✓ Adjust arc and throw on rotors and pop-up sprays for full coverage
  • ✓ Inspect the manifold and valve box for cracks, leaks, and solenoid faults
  • ✓ Verify the Hydrawise (or other smart controller) Wi-Fi connection and program
  • ✓ Set the seasonal watering schedule against Newmarket's clay-loam soil profile
  • ✓ Confirm rain-sensor function (where installed) — required for water-bylaw compliance
  • ✓ Flag any backflow assembly issues for referral to a certified Ontario backflow tester
  • ✓ Walk you through the system and answer any questions on the spot

Backflow testing is a separately-licensed trade in Ontario. PJL does not service backflow assemblies but can refer you to a certified tester if yours needs annual recertification.

Newmarket Sprinkler FAQs

When should I open my sprinkler system in Newmarket?

In Newmarket, the safe window for spring opening is typically the second week of April through mid-May, after the last hard frost. Opening too early risks freeze damage to backflow assemblies and exposed risers. We start booking in late March and prioritize repeat customers first — book early to lock in your preferred week.

What does sprinkler repair cost in Newmarket?

Our service call (mobilization + on-site assessment) is $95. Repair labour is billed at $95 from there. Common parts costs: sprinkler head replacement $68, pipe break repair from $120, full 3-valve manifold rebuild starting at $135 plus valves. If our AI-Smart-Intake diagnostic tool correctly predicts the fix, you get one hour of repair labour free on the diagnosed work — our only standing discount.

Do you offer same-day sprinkler repair in Newmarket?

Yes — during the active irrigation season (mid-April to mid-October), we hold same-day repair slots for Newmarket addresses booked before 11 AM, subject to availability. Outside that window, we book within 24–48 hours.

Are there watering bylaws in Newmarket I need to know about?

Newmarket falls under the Town of Newmarket's water conservation bylaw, which restricts lawn watering to odd/even calendar days (matching your house number) between May 1 and September 30, typically before 10 AM or after 7 PM. Your sprinkler controller should be programmed to comply — we set this automatically during spring opening if you let us know your address parity.

How deep do sprinkler lines need to be buried in Ontario?

The Ontario frost line is approximately 1.2 metres (4 feet) in the York Region, but irrigation lines don't need to be buried that deep — they're drained and pressurized only during the warm months. PJL buries mainlines 8–12 inches deep and laterals 6–8 inches deep, which is standard for the GTA. Fall winterization (compressor blowout) is what protects them from freeze damage, not depth.

Can you upgrade my existing system to a Hydrawise smart controller?

Yes — Hydrawise retrofits are one of our specialties. Patrick is Hunter-certified through Hunter University, and we install Hunter HPC-400 Hydrawise WiFi controllers on existing systems regularly. Pricing: $595 for 1–4 zones, $750 for 5–7 zones, $1,195 for 8–16 zones, all-in. Typical install takes under 90 minutes.

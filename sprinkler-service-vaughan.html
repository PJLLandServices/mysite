Vaughan irrigation — a city of distinct neighborhoods, each with different needs.

Vaughan isn't really one place. Maple has the new builds and tight subdivision lots. Kleinburg has the estates and acreage. What stays the same across all of Vaughan is the heavy York Region clay soil (slow water absorption, needs cycle & soak programming) and the City of Vaughan watering-restriction bylaws that smart controllers like Hydrawise handle automatically.

Vaughan watering bylaws — your sprinkler should follow them automatically

Vaughan typically restricts lawn watering to alternate days based on house number (odd/even) during summer water-conservation periods. Old timer-based controllers don't know about this — they just keep running the schedule you set. Hydrawise, programmed properly, automatically respects municipal watering windows so you stay compliant without thinking about it. We set this up on every Vaughan install as standard.

What's Included in a PJL Spring Opening — Vaughan

Every spring opening in Vaughan includes a full 10-point system check before we leave your property:

  • ✓ Slow-pressurize the main supply line to prevent water-hammer damage to PVC fittings
  • ✓ Test each zone individually for correct pressure, coverage, and rotation
  • ✓ Check every sprinkler head for winter damage, misalignment, or clogged nozzles
  • ✓ Adjust arc and throw on rotors and pop-up sprays for full coverage
  • ✓ Inspect the manifold and valve box for cracks, leaks, and solenoid faults
  • ✓ Verify the Hydrawise (or other smart controller) Wi-Fi connection and program
  • ✓ Set the seasonal watering schedule against Vaughan's clay-loam soil profile
  • ✓ Confirm rain-sensor function (where installed) — required for water-bylaw compliance
  • ✓ Flag any backflow assembly issues for referral to a certified Ontario backflow tester
  • ✓ Walk you through the system and answer any questions on the spot

Backflow testing is a separately-licensed trade in Ontario. PJL does not service backflow assemblies but can refer you to a certified tester if yours needs annual recertification.

Vaughan Sprinkler FAQs

When should I open my sprinkler system in Vaughan?

In Vaughan, the safe window for spring opening is typically the second week of April through mid-May, after the last hard frost. Opening too early risks freeze damage to backflow assemblies and exposed risers. We start booking in late March and prioritize repeat customers first — book early to lock in your preferred week.

What does sprinkler repair cost in Vaughan?

Our service call (mobilization + on-site assessment) is $95. Repair labour is billed at $95 from there. Common parts costs: sprinkler head replacement $68, pipe break repair from $120, full 3-valve manifold rebuild starting at $135 plus valves. If our AI-Smart-Intake diagnostic tool correctly predicts the fix, you get one hour of repair labour free on the diagnosed work — our only standing discount.

Do you offer same-day sprinkler repair in Vaughan?

Yes — during the active irrigation season (mid-April to mid-October), we hold same-day repair slots for Vaughan addresses booked before 11 AM, subject to availability. Most Vaughan calls land within 4–6 hours of dispatch.

Are there watering bylaws in Vaughan I need to know about?

Vaughan falls under York Region's Outdoor Water-Use Bylaw. From May 15 to September 30, watering is permitted on odd or even calendar days matching your house number, between 6 a.m. and 9 a.m. and 6 p.m. and 9 p.m. New sod and seed get a one-month grace period. We program your controller to comply automatically during spring opening.

How deep do sprinkler lines need to be buried in Ontario?

The Ontario frost line in York Region is approximately 1.2 metres (4 feet), but irrigation lines don't need to be buried that deep — they're drained and pressurized only during the warm months. PJL buries mainlines 8–12 inches deep and laterals 6–8 inches deep, which is standard for the GTA. Fall winterization (compressor blowout) is what protects them from freeze damage, not depth.

Can you upgrade my existing system to a Hydrawise smart controller?

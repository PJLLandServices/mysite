What to do with your sprinkler timer over winter.

PLPatrick Lalande •September 2026•5 min read•📍 Newmarket, Aurora & the GTA

In this article

  • How to turn it off
  • If a zone runs anyway

Once the lines are blown out, the timer in your garage is the last piece of the sprinkler system still switched on. Should you unplug it? Leave it? Does it matter? It does, a little — mostly because of what happens when someone fiddles with it in February.

Should I unplug my sprinkler timer for the winter?

Quick answer

No. Leave it plugged in and set to Off (or winter mode on Hydrawise). Hunter recommends exactly this: power on, dial off. It keeps settings ready for spring, and on outdoor timers the small amount of heat helps keep condensation out.

That's straight from the manufacturer — see Hunter's winterization guide (opens in a new tab).

Unplugging a Hydrawise controller just knocks it offline all winter. When it comes back in spring, you may be chasing Wi-Fi problems before the season even starts. Older timers that lose the clock when unplugged will need the time and date reset.

The exception: if the timer is outdoors and exposed, or the cabinet is damaged and water gets in, have it looked at.

How do I turn off my sprinkler timer for the winter?

Quick answer

Set the timer to its Off position rather than deleting the program. On most dial timers that means turning the dial to Off or System Off. On Hydrawise, the controller goes into winter mode. Your watering schedule stays saved for spring.

If your system was closed by our team, this is already done. Setting the controller is part of every closing. Here's what it looks like by brand, in case you need to check or someone changed it.

Hydrawise (Hunter HC, Pro-HC, HPC)

We set Hydrawise controllers to winter mode during the closing. From the app, the Actions menu is where you suspend watering or turn the controller off. Leave it plugged in and on Wi-Fi.

If you change your router or Wi-Fi password over the winter, reconnect the controller before spring: Connect Your Hydrawise Controller to a New Wi-Fi Network. If the app says it's offline, see Hydrawise Controller Offline?

Hunter X-Core and Pro-C

Turn the dial to System Off. The program stays stored. In spring, turn the dial back to Run. If it waters at odd times or runs extra cycles next season, the programming guides walk through it: Hunter X-Core · Hunter Pro-C · Hunter SRC.

Rain Bird ESP-TM2

Turn the dial to Off. Your schedule is kept. See How to Program a Rain Bird ESP-TM2 for the spring reset.

Orbit dial timers

Most Orbit Easy-Set and Easy Dial timers have an Off position on the dial. Use that. See How to Program an Orbit Sprinkler Timer.

What happens if the timer runs a zone after the closing?

Quick answer

If the main water supply is shut off, nothing comes out, and a short run won't hurt anything. The damage happens when someone turns the water back on to test a zone. That refills the blown-out pipes, and the water freezes in them.

So the timer setting is really a backup. The real protection is the main shut-off staying closed. Don't turn the water back on to the sprinkler system until spring start-up, even on a warm week in November. For everything we leave in which position, see Fall Sprinkler Closing: How to Prepare & What We Do.

Do rain sensors or flow sensors need anything for winter?

Quick answer

Usually not. Rain and freeze sensors can stay mounted. The lines feeding a flow sensor are cleared with the rest of the system during the closing. If a wireless sensor has a battery, winter is a good time to note it for replacement in spring.

Will my old timer make it through another season?

A timer that keeps losing its schedule, runs at the wrong time, or has a cracked cabinet is worth replacing — and winter is the easiest time to decide, before the spring rush. A Hydrawise controller adjusts watering to the local weather, alerts your phone when a zone misbehaves, and can save a typical Newmarket property up to $580/year in water.

As a Hydrawise Authorized Installer, we can swap it at your spring opening. See Hydrawise smart controllers.

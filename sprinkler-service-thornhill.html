What's Included in a PJL Spring Opening — Thornhill

Every spring opening in Thornhill includes a full 10-point system check before we leave your property:

  • ✓ Slow-pressurize the main supply line to prevent water-hammer damage to PVC fittings
  • ✓ Test each zone individually for correct pressure, coverage, and rotation
  • ✓ Check every sprinkler head for winter damage, misalignment, or clogged nozzles
  • ✓ Adjust arc and throw on rotors and pop-up sprays for full coverage
  • ✓ Inspect the manifold and valve box for cracks, leaks, and solenoid faults
  • ✓ Verify the Hydrawise (or other smart controller) Wi-Fi connection and program
  • ✓ Set the seasonal watering schedule against Thornhill's clay-loam soil profile
  • ✓ Confirm rain-sensor function (where installed) — required for water-bylaw compliance
  • ✓ Flag any backflow assembly issues for referral to a certified Ontario backflow tester
  • ✓ Walk you through the system and answer any questions on the spot

Backflow testing is a separately-licensed trade in Ontario. PJL does not service backflow assemblies but can refer you to a certified tester if yours needs annual recertification.

Thornhill Sprinkler FAQs

When should I open my sprinkler system in Thornhill?

In Thornhill, the safe window for spring opening is typically the second week of April through mid-May, after the last hard frost. Opening too early risks freeze damage to backflow assemblies and exposed risers. We start booking in late March and prioritize repeat customers first — book early to lock in your preferred week.

What does sprinkler repair cost in Thornhill?

Our service call (mobilization + on-site assessment) is $95. Repair labour is billed at $95 from there. Common parts costs: sprinkler head replacement $68, pipe break repair from $120, full 3-valve manifold rebuild starting at $135 plus valves. Are there watering bylaws in Thornhill I need to know about?

Thornhill straddles two York Region municipalities — properties west of Yonge Street are inside Vaughan, properties east are inside Markham — and the bylaws differ slightly. Vaughan permits watering 6 a.m.–9 a.m. and 6 p.m.–9 p.m. on odd/even days matching your house number (May 15 to September 30). Markham permits either 6 a.m.–9 a.m. OR 6 p.m.–9 p.m. (not both) on odd/even days, June 1 to September 30. Both follow the odd/even house-number rule. We confirm which side of Yonge you're on during spring opening and program the controller accordingly.

How deep do sprinkler lines need to be buried in Ontario?

The Ontario frost line in York Region is approximately 1.2 metres (4 feet), but irrigation lines don't need to be buried that deep — they're drained and pressurized only during the warm months. PJL buries mainlines 8–12 inches deep and laterals 6–8 inches deep, which is standard for the GTA. Fall winterization (compressor blowout) is what protects them from freeze damage, not depth.

Can you upgrade my existing system to a Hydrawise smart controller?

Yes — Hydrawise retrofits are one of our specialties. Patrick is Hunter-certified through Hunter University, and we install Hunter HPC-400 Hydrawise WiFi controllers on existing systems regularly. Pricing: $595 for 1–4 zones, $750 for 5–7 zones, $1,195 for 8–16 zones, all-in. Typical install takes under 90 minutes.

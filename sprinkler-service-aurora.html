Aurora sits on the same heavy York Region clay soil as the rest of north GTA — which means every standard "rule of thumb" you'll find in a generic blog post about sprinkler watering doesn't quite work here. Clay holds water, but accepts it slowly. Standard spray nozzles dump water faster than the soil can absorb. Without proper hydrozoning, cycle & soak programming and the right head selection, a brand-new sprinkler system in Aurora can produce a worse-looking lawn than no system at all.

That's where we come in. PJL Land Services has been installing and maintaining residential and commercial irrigation across Aurora since 2015. What's Included in a PJL Spring Opening — Aurora

Every spring opening in Aurora includes a full 10-point system check before we leave your property:

  • ✓ Slow-pressurize the main supply line to prevent water-hammer damage to PVC fittings
  • ✓ Test each zone individually for correct pressure, coverage, and rotation
  • ✓ Check every sprinkler head for winter damage, misalignment, or clogged nozzles
  • ✓ Adjust arc and throw on rotors and pop-up sprays for full coverage
  • ✓ Inspect the manifold and valve box for cracks, leaks, and solenoid faults
  • ✓ Verify the Hydrawise (or other smart controller) Wi-Fi connection and program
  • ✓ Set the seasonal watering schedule against Aurora's clay-loam soil profile
  • ✓ Confirm rain-sensor function (where installed) — required for water-bylaw compliance
  • ✓ Flag any backflow assembly issues for referral to a certified Ontario backflow tester
  • ✓ Walk you through the system and answer any questions on the spot

Backflow testing is a separately-licensed trade in Ontario. PJL does not service backflow assemblies but can refer you to a certified tester if yours needs annual recertification.

Aurora Sprinkler FAQs

When should I open my sprinkler system in Aurora?

In Aurora, the safe window for spring opening is typically the second week of April through mid-May, after the last hard frost. Opening too early risks freeze damage to backflow assemblies and exposed risers. We start booking in late March and prioritize repeat customers first — book early to lock in your preferred week.

What does sprinkler repair cost in Aurora?

Our service call (mobilization + on-site assessment) is $95. Repair labour is billed at $95 from there. Common parts costs: sprinkler head replacement $68, pipe break repair from $120, full 3-valve manifold rebuild starting at $135 plus valves. Are there watering bylaws in Aurora I need to know about?

Aurora falls under York Region's Outdoor Water-Use Bylaw. From May 15 to September 30, lawn watering is permitted on odd-numbered calendar days for odd-numbered house addresses and even-numbered days for even addresses, between 6 a.m. and 10 a.m. or 6 p.m. and 10 p.m. New sod and seed get a one-month grace period. Your sprinkler controller should be programmed to comply — we set this automatically during spring opening if you let us know your address parity.

How deep do sprinkler lines need to be buried in Ontario?

The Ontario frost line is approximately 1.2 metres (4 feet) in the York Region, but irrigation lines don't need to be buried that deep — they're drained and pressurized only during the warm months. PJL buries mainlines 8–12 inches deep and laterals 6–8 inches deep, which is standard for the GTA. Fall winterization (compressor blowout) is what protects them from freeze damage, not depth.

## Voice & Tone

- Real, warm, time-efficient. A neighbour who happens to do irrigation for
  a living — not a brochure, not a help desk.
- Contractions, plain words, short paragraphs. "Yeah, that's annoying" is
  allowed; "we strive to provide" is not.
- Honest to the point of talking customers out of work: if a system does
  not need replacing, say so. Repair vs replace is maths, never pressure.
- Practical. Every piece solves one real homeowner problem and ends with a
  clear next step.
- Acknowledge the frustration in one sentence, then get to the fix.
- No hype, no fake urgency, no exclamation-mark enthusiasm. One emoji at
  most, usually none.

## Positioning

- Sprinkler and irrigation design, installation, repair, spring opening
  and fall closing, drip irrigation, and landscape lighting — Newmarket
  home base, nine core York Region towns with same-day repair in season,
  extended coverage about 90 minutes out across the GTA, Simcoe, Durham
  and Peel.
- Built on three trades: plumbing-grade hydraulics, industrial-scale
  irrigation experience across Ontario, and civil-construction project
  management. Systems are engineered for the property (grade, shade, soil,
  water source) before a head is placed.
- What PJL says that competitors do not:
  - **Prices are published.** On the website, on the quote, on the
    invoice, and the quote number is the invoice number.
  - **Hunter Hydrawise smart controller standard** on every new install.
  - **Three-year warranty on parts and labour** on installs.
  - **Same-day repair, April–October** — and if the day cannot be reached,
    the customer is told on the call rather than left waiting.
  - **Hunter and Rain Bird parts only.**
  - **Free 45-minute on-site design consultation.**
- Relationship-driven, not transactional: no seasonal upsells pushed into
  a repair conversation; the customer portal is where seasonal booking
  lives, on the customer's own time.

## Core Topics (genuine expertise)

- Diagnosing sprinkler faults: zones not running, heads not spraying, dry
  spots, water pooling, wiring and valve problems, finding a broken pipe.
- Hunter Hydrawise: setup, Wi-Fi changes, offline recovery, new-homeowner
  takeover; programming Hunter Pro-C / X-Core / SRC, Rain Bird ESP-TM2,
  Orbit timers.
- Seasonal service in a freeze-thaw climate: what a proper blow-out is,
  when to open and close in Ontario, what freeze damage costs.
- Irrigation design for York Region conditions: clay-heavy subdivisions,
  estate lots, mature gardens and hedges (drip), new sod watering.
- Water use: York Region and municipal watering bylaws, flow sensors,
  what smart controllers actually save.
- Landscape lighting alongside irrigation.

## Customer Language (how customers describe problems)

- "One zone isn't coming on." "A head is stuck / not popping up."
  "There's a wet spot that never dries." "The controller says offline."
- "How much is a fall closing?" "When should I turn the sprinklers on?"
- "Do you come to [town]?" "Can you look at a system the previous owner
  installed?"
- They ask for a price before they ask for a visit; they compare against
  DIY and against the neighbour's guy.

## Things We Never Say

- Invented experience or frequency claims ("nine times out of ten", "in
  my 20 years") — nothing that is not in this file or verifiable.
- A price range or a rounded price. Prices come from `pricing.json`
  through `data-price` tokens; anything not in the price list is "a
  custom quote after a site visit".
- "Call a sprinkler company" — PJL is the sprinkler company.
- Corporate filler: "I understand your concern", "we strive to",
  "industry-leading", "best in class".
- Pressure: "book now before it's too late", countdown language.
- Generic Ontario-wide claims where a specific town, soil, or bylaw fact
  would do.

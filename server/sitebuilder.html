1 · Water Supply

Bucket test: fill a 5-gal bucket, note seconds → GPM = 300 ÷ seconds. Heads/zone = ceiling ÷ flow-per-head. Note: a PGP rotor flows the same GPM at 90° or 360° — arc changes coverage & precip rate, not flow.
Water supply is not accurately measured unless a new water-supply installation is required — current available flow is based on a calculated formula. Additional zones (at additional cost) may be associated with under-performing supplies.

4 · Water Cost by Town

Add an area above to see water costs.
This water-cost estimate saves as its own document in the project folder (separate from the system design). Rates are the combined water + sewer volumetric charge on a standard residential meter (no irrigation deduct meter). GPM figures are US gallons (1 US gal = 0.00378541 m³). Blank run-time = the default above; type a number to override one zone. Drip zones are included at their computed flow (they run far longer at low GPM — set a realistic per-zone run time).

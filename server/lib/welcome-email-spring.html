Thank you for choosing PJL Land Services.
Finding a sprinkler company you can count on is harder than it should be. Calls go unanswered, appointments slip, and nobody explains what was done. You’ve made the right call, and we don’t take that trust lightly. Here’s exactly how we run, so you know what to expect from the first visit on.
HOW YOUR BOOKING WAS MADE
Our booking system routes our trucks by area. It places streets and subdivisions in sequence, so the day runs in order instead of criss-crossing York Region and the GTA. Keeping two homes on the same street is just plain smart.
It’s also how we keep your price where it is. Less driving means lower costs, and that saving stays with you.
Returning customers don’t book at all. We place their property on a route day ourselves and send them the date by email and text, with a link to their own appointment page. From that link, they can move the day, cancel it, or tell us to come whenever we’re in the area. A simple acceptance confirms your spot, and we’ll see you on that day! We’ll text you the day before as a reminder too.
Your future spring openings
We start openings when the weather allows, not before. Once the season shows us when we can begin, we set the schedule, with the goal of every system running by the May 24 long weekend.
Every fall closing includes an in-depth inspection of your system. We document anything we find, but repairs wait until spring, when the system is running and we can actually fix and test it. That’s what the spring opening is for: we quote any repairs on site, and for the most part complete them the same day. If your system needs more attention than one visit allows, our technicians isolate the larger issues so the rest of your system keeps running until we’re back to finish the job.
Every visit is documented
Our technicians work with trade-designed software and field applications that create the documentation you receive, and that we keep for future services. This allows for dedicated service reports on the technician’s work, your property’s important components, and your sprinkler system’s layout. Our technicians record technical information such as shut-offs, blow-outs, valves, zone locations, zone variations and coverage types.
So when you call about a dry corner out back, you don’t have to describe it. We already know which zone that is.
YOUR YEAR WITH US
Summer
In summer, our team is available for whatever your system may throw at you. Often, repairs can be completed the same day. Looking to make some changes to your sprinkler system, or unsure about coverage issues from new plant growth? Reach out to us anytime, and we’ll arrive and source solutions.
 
Fall
Fall closings are part of the same automated booking system. Areas and days are grouped together, and bookings are delivered by email and text message. A simple acceptance confirms your spot, and a text message the day before reminds you of the appointment. Often, fall closings can be done without you needing to be home.
 
All seasonal appointments receive the same link, where you can change your appointment up to 24 hours in advance.
Planning a landscape renovation?
Planning is the part of the job we value most. Once you have your landscape plan in hand, call us before the work begins. We’ll come out and walk the property with you, plan alongside the project, and make sure the irrigation is ready for your new landscape. It’s far easier, and costs less, than working around finished work afterwards.

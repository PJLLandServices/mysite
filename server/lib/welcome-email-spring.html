Thank you for choosing PJL Land Services.
Finding a sprinkler company you can count on is harder than it should be. Calls go unanswered, appointments slip, and nobody explains what was done. You’ve made the right call, and we don’t take that trust lightly. Here’s exactly how we run, so you know what to expect from the first visit on.
HOW YOUR BOOKING WAS MADE
 
This is the only time you’ll need to book. From next season on, we’ll put you on the schedule ourselves and send you the date by email and text, with a link to your own appointment page.
From that link, you can move the day, cancel it, or tell us to come whenever we’re in the area. A simple acceptance confirms your spot, and we’ll text you the day before as a reminder. You’ll get a morning window, 8am to 12pm, or an afternoon window, 12pm to 5pm.
Your future spring openings
We start openings once the frost is out of the ground, never before.
Every system is running by the May 24 long weekend.
On the visit, we turn your water on, start the controller, and run every zone on its own to check coverage, pressure and operation. Anything found gets quoted and fixed on the spot where possible, including anything already noted on your fall closing, since that carries forward onto this visit.
Every visit is documented
Our technicians work with trade-designed software and field applications that create the documentation you receive, and that we keep for future services. This allows for dedicated service reports on the technician’s work, your property’s important components, and your sprinkler system’s layout. Our technicians record technical information such as shut-offs, blow-outs, valves, zone locations, zone variations and coverage types.
So when you call about a dry corner out back, you don’t have to describe it. We already know which zone that is.
YOUR YEAR WITH US
Summer
In summer, our team is available for whatever your system may throw at you. Often, repairs can be completed the same day. Looking to make some changes to your sprinkler system, or unsure about coverage issues from new plant growth? Reach out to us anytime, and we’ll arrive and source solutions.
 
Fall
Fall closings run through the same booking, starting from mid-September, and we work through the season until every system on the board is done. We’ll send you the date by email and text, a simple acceptance confirms your spot, and we’ll text you the day before as a reminder.
Planning a landscape renovation?
Planning is the part of the job we value most. Once you have your landscape plan in hand, call us before the work begins. We’ll come out and walk the property with you, plan alongside the project, and make sure the irrigation is ready for your new landscape. It’s far easier, and costs less, than working around finished work afterwards.

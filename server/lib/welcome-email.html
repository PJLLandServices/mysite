HOW YOUR BOOKING GOT MADE
Our booking system routes trucks by area. It sequences streets and subdivisions so the day runs in order instead of criss-crossing the region. Two homes on the same street is just plain smart.
That’s not housekeeping — it’s the reason our prices sit where they do. Tight routes across York Region and the GTA mean less fuel and less windshield time, and that saving goes back into what we charge you.
Online booking is how new customers get started. From your second season on you won’t have to book at all — your property goes onto a route day in the season plan, and the appointment comes to you by email and text with a link to your own appointment page.
What we promise is a half-day, never a minute. Morning means an 8am to 12pm arrival window, afternoon means 12pm to 5pm. The exact minute belongs to the route and shifts as the day gets tuned, so you’ll never be given a time that changes on you later.
From your appointment page you can confirm, move to another open day, cancel, correct your zone count, or tell us to come whenever we’re in the area. The first move is yours to make — no phone call, no waiting on us. After that, or inside 24 hours of the visit, give us a ring instead: by then you’re already sequenced into a day’s route.
You don’t have to reply at all. Nobody gets un-booked for staying quiet — the appointment stands unless you cancel or move it. Either way you’ll get a reminder text the day before.

YOUR YEAR WITH US

You’ll hear from us ahead of each season. Not a newsletter — just what you need to plan around.
Spring — openings
We start when the weather lets us, not before — turning a system on into a frost does damage. Once the season shows its hand we set the schedule and book everyone in. The goal is every system open by the May long weekend.
Small repairs get handled during the opening. Anything large gets leapfrogged and rebooked, so one big job doesn’t hold up the street behind it. If a major repair comes up outside the opening, we isolate the fault rather than shutting your whole system down, and come back with the time to do it properly.
 
Summer — service
We’re available for whatever the system throws at you. Repairs are usually same-day. And if you’ve got thoughts about changing something — a zone that never worked right, a bed you want covered — book a site meeting. It’s free. We’ll walk it with you and tell you honestly what direction the system should take.
 
Fall — closings
Booking opens mid-September and runs on the same footing as spring: a date and a time window. Everything has to be closed out before a hard freeze — that deadline is set by the weather, not by us, which is why we book the whole region in sequence.
A closing is a quick job and our technicians could do it in their sleep — we still treat it seriously. On properties we know, it’s done entirely from outside and you don’t need to be home. We confirm that’s possible at your first visit, once we’ve been through the system and know where everything is.
Planning a landscape renovation?
Call us once you’ve got the landscape plan in hand and we’ll plan around it, working alongside the build so the irrigation is right the first time.
Bringing us in afterwards means cutting back into finished work, and that always costs more than planning it in from the start. Planning is the value we’re proudest of, and it’s worth nothing if we hear about the job too late.
